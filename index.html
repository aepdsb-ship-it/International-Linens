International Linens: Reliable Hospital and Hotel Linen Manufacturing

Quality linens play an important role in hospitals, hotels, restaurants, and other institutional environments. These businesses need fabrics that offer comfort, durability, hygiene, and consistent performance through frequent commercial washing. International Linens is a Pakistan-based manufacturer and exporter providing hospital, hotel, healthcare, and restaurant linens for institutional buyers around the world.

A Trusted Linen Manufacturer Since 2004

International Linens began its journey in 2004 with a small stitching operation and has developed into a vertically integrated textile manufacturer based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. The company manages multiple stages of production, including yarn sourcing, weaving, knitting, processing, cutting, stitching, packing, and shipping. This integrated approach allows greater control over quality and production consistency.

Hospital and Healthcare Linens

Healthcare facilities require linens that can withstand repeated industrial laundering while maintaining their structure and comfort. International Linens manufactures hospital bed sheets, fitted sheets, flat sheets, pillowcases, blankets, patient gowns, scrub suits, and pediatric apparel. These products are designed for hospitals, clinics, healthcare laundries, and other medical environments.

The company also offers specialized products such as knitted fitted sheets and hospital blankets, providing options for different institutional requirements.

Hotel and Hospitality Products

Hotels and resorts need bedding that combines guest comfort with long-term durability. International Linens produces hotel bedding, blankets, pillowcases, and other hospitality textiles in consistent specifications. Products can also be developed according to customer requirements, making the manufacturer suitable for distributors and private-label businesses.

Restaurant and Food-Service Linens

Beyond healthcare and hospitality, International Linens supplies dining textiles for restaurants, catering companies, and food-service businesses. Its range includes tablecloths, napkins, and other commercial-use textiles manufactured and finished in-house.

Quality, Manufacturing, and Global Supply

International Linens focuses on quality control throughout its manufacturing process. Its website highlights certifications and audits including OEKO-TEX Standard 100, ISO 9001:2015, SEDEX/SMETA, and C-TPAT. The company also supplies international B2B buyers across markets including the United States, United Kingdom, Europe, Canada, and Australia.

For businesses searching for a dependable institutional linen manufacturer, International Linens offers factory-direct production, custom specifications, private-label options, and export-ready supply. Its combination of manufacturing expertise and quality-focused production makes it a practical partner for healthcare, hospitality, and food-service linen programs.
